# Testing

Run `python tools/run_release_gates.py` from the repository root. It checks that the import map matches the module tree, runs `node --check` on every module, then runs each `tests/*-regression.mjs` through the alias loader. `tests/sortie-regression.mjs` covers sector structure, sortie start and end, XP and level-ups, card offers and caps, additive stacking, relics, pickups, salvage banking, contracts and unlocks, the Workshop, ships, revives and save round-trips.

`node --experimental-loader ./tests/loader.mjs tests/progression-sim.mjs 40 all 1` plays 40 sorties from a fresh save (spending salvage on the cheapest Workshop upgrade) and reports pacing; pass `nomedals` to switch achievements off, and dodge 0 for a weaker pilot. `node tools/profile.mjs stress 8` prints a CPU profile of a busy late wave. For balance, run the headless bot (add a threat level as a seventh argument to test Threat): `node --experimental-loader ./tests/loader.mjs ./tests/balance-sim.mjs 4 0 vanguard fresh 0`. Arguments are runs, Workshop level, ship, unlock set (`fresh`, `some`, `all`) and autopilot dodge skill (0-3). At v2.0.0 a fresh pilot typically falls between waves 20 and 33, Workshop 3 around wave 40, Workshop 6 around 50, and a maxed Workshop reaches the wave 60 Singularity.

For browser verification, serve the root over HTTP at phone widths (320px, 390px) and on desktop. Confirm: the Hangar shows the ship above the Launch card and the Launch button stays in reach; a sortie starts with the cannon and Overdrive; waves spawn and the ship auto-fires; XP orbs and salvage fly to the ship; a level-up pauses combat with three cards and a pick updates the loadout strip; the ability button shows its cooldown; clearing wave 10 offers relics; death shows the debrief with banked salvage and completed contracts; Workshop purchases, ship purchase and selection, and the Armory and Contracts screens update correctly; pause, settings and abandon work. Use `?debug=1` only in its isolated sandbox. `node tools/shoot.mjs ../shots` captures the scenes defined in `modules/ui/debug.js` (launch, missions, workshop, armory, armory:locked, ships, contracts, records, awards, banner:<id>, bannerfly:<id>, bannershow, bannershow:fly, bannershow:legendary, bannershow:legendary:ships, hull:<ship>, hullfly:<ship>, route, fusion, levelup, relic, notice, medal, pause, loadout, debrief, stress, paint:<id>) at 390×844 through headless Chrome.
